03 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few straight answers.

The creative part should feel open. The working relationship should feel clear.

01Who owns the rights?

You do. Your story and finished custom book remain yours. Any public use—such as a portfolio case study—would be handled with your permission.

02How long does a book take?

The timeline is set at kickoff. It depends on the length of the book, the condition of the source material, and how quickly approvals come back.

03How do revisions work?

The approval gates define the scope. We approve the storyline before likeness work, likeness before full spreads, and spreads before print. Reopening an earlier approved stage can affect schedule and cost, so changes are handled where they belong.
