# Backyard at Dusk — Locked Scene Recipe (BookVizion v6 concept samples, drafted 2026-10-18)

CONCEPT SAMPLE SCENE — this scene is rendered once per house style (Set A, the stage set) with identical composition in every version. Only the style medium changes. Every use is labeled CONCEPT SAMPLE.

## The scene
A backyard at dusk on a clear, dry evening. Maya sits on a low wooden garden step reading a picture book to Biscuit, who sits beside her. Fireflies drift in the air. A fence runs horizontally behind them strung with warm string lights. The upper third of the frame is a deep blue-green dusk sky. Warm light falls on the two subjects against the cool dusk.

## The moment (locked — Q19)
THE SMILE MOMENT. The picture book is open in Maya's lap; her head is turned toward Biscuit and she is smiling at him — the gap-tooth smile, delighted and warm. Biscuit sits upright beside her, head turned up toward her, attentive. It is NOT a reading-aloud pose: her eyes are on the cat, not on the page. This exact moment holds in all six style versions.

## Camera
Eye-level camera, at the seated subjects' height — never looking down on them, never a low heroic angle. Medium-wide shot: both subjects full-body with the step, a band of garden ground below, the fence line behind, and sky above. 4:3 stage framing (2400×1800 master). No tilt, no dutch angle, no fisheye or lens distortion. The subjects sit three-quarters toward camera, about 30 degrees off front — NOT in profile; both of Maya's eyes, her gap tooth, and both puffs stay visible.

## Composition locks (identical in every style)
- Maya LEFT-CENTER, seated on the garden step, full body in frame.
- Biscuit RIGHT of Maya, ON the step beside her, smaller in scale, full body in frame; his head sits at roughly 55–60% of frame height.
- Fence line runs HORIZONTALLY behind them at roughly the vertical midpoint.
- Deep blue-green dusk sky fills the UPPER THIRD of the frame.
- String lights trace the fence line behind the subjects.
- Fireflies: 5 to 12 only, scattered in the mid-ground air, small warm points, never swarming the faces and kept clear of Biscuit's collar-tag zone.
- Subjects occupy the warm foreground pool of light; the background stays cooler and quieter.
- No text space is reserved — this is stage art, not a book spread; no copy is set on it.

## Lighting
Key light: WARM, from the LOWER-LEFT (Pixel's proposal, adopted) — as if from a porch light or lantern just out of frame — raking gently up across Maya and Biscuit, warm highlights on her raincoat and his orange coat. Ambient fill is the cool blue-green dusk. String lights and fireflies are small warm accents, not light sources. The subjects must read as the warm focal point against the cool background in every style. No harsh shadows, no spotlight cone, no lightning, no moon dominating the sky.

## Props list (complete — nothing else appears)
- Low wooden garden step (dry, plain wood).
- One picture book, open, in Maya's lap — its cover is DEEP WARM RED and blank (locked in every style); pages blank of readable text, simple shapes only.
- Wooden backyard fence with one string of warm bulb lights.
- Grass/garden ground, simple and quiet, a few soft plant shapes at the edges.
- Fireflies (ambient, see composition locks).
Wardrobe and character details come from maya-recipe.md and biscuit-recipe.md and are not restated or altered here. Maya's raincoat hood stays DOWN. No other props, furniture, toys, houses, vehicles, or people.

## Weather and surface state
Clear dry evening. Everything is DRY: the step, the ground, the raincoat. The raincoat is Maya's signature wardrobe, not weather storytelling — there is no rain, no wet sheen, no puddles, no drips, no umbrellas. Fireflies mean a clear night; keep it one.

## NEGATIVES (Folio-style — every render, every style)
No text, lettering, words, logos, or watermark anywhere, including on the book pages and cover. No photorealism, no photograph look, no realistic skin texture or individual-hair rendering. No extra characters — no other children, adults, animals, birds, or background figures. No rain, wet surfaces, puddles, drips, umbrellas, or storm clouds. No changed wardrobe: raincoat never a different color, hood never up, boots never a different color, tee stripes never recolored. No missing collar on the cat, no extra tag, no solid (unringed) tail. No border, frame, vignette, paper edge, or matte around the image. No daytime sky, no bright blue sky, no sunset orange sky — deep blue-green dusk only. No moon as a dominant element, no stars as the main sky feature. No reading-aloud pose (her eyes stay on the cat). No lens blur, bokeh, or depth of field — subjects separate from the background by value and rim light only. Biscuit never long-haired, never seal-point or colorpoint. No composition drift: girl never right of frame, cat never left of her, fence never diagonal, sky never less than the upper third.
