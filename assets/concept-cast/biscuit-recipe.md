# Biscuit — Definitive Companion Character Recipe (BookVizion v6 concept samples, drafted 2026-10-18)

CONCEPT SAMPLE CHARACTER — Biscuit is an invented cat created for BookVizion concept samples only. He is not a real pet and is not a client animal. Every render of Biscuit is labeled CONCEPT SAMPLE in use.

Species/Breed: Biscuit is a small adult MALE domestic shorthair cat, an orange tabby, and Maya's companion in the BookVizion concept samples. Preserve this recipe's likeness exactly in every render; only the art style medium changes between renders, never his markings, collar, or build.

Colors: Warm orange tabby coat with slightly deeper orange striping. Chest and front paws are clean white. Eyes are amber, gently enlarged in the canonical cartoon proportion, with dark pupils and small bright catchlights. Nose is soft pink; inner ears are muted warm pink. Collar is leaf green with a round gold tag. Keep the coat warm orange — never red, never brown, never cream or white overall.

Markings: Mackerel tabby striping (narrow stripes, never blotched classic swirls) on the head, back, legs, and tail, kept soft and grouped rather than drawn stripe by stripe. Muzzle and chin are white, continuous with the chest bib. A soft tabby “M” sits on the forehead.

Species check: domestic cat — four legs, whiskered muzzle, tail; never long-haired, never colorpoint or seal-point. White chest bib covering the chest and throat. White front paws (both, fully white from the wrist down). Hind paws stay orange. The tail carries clear darker orange rings along its length — a striped tail in every pose, never a solid tail. Match this distribution in every render.

Signature features: Amber eyes gently enlarged in cartoon proportion, pink nose, white chest bib, white front paws, ringed striped tail, and the green collar with its round gold tag worn in EVERY render — the collar is part of his identity, not an optional accessory. Medium triangular ears, fine white whiskers, small neat face. No oversized ears, long decorative eyelashes, or altered eye color.

Proportions/Build: Small adult cat with a light, neat build — noticeably smaller than a large Ragdoll-type cat. Naturally sized medium triangular ears, balanced head, short smooth coat. Tail is medium length and slim with visible rings, carried in a gentle curve or upright with a soft tip bend. Do not turn him into a kitten, a large plush longhair, or a chubby cartoon cat.

Collar/Tags/Accessories: Leaf-green collar with ONE round gold tag, worn always. The tag is blank — no engraving, letters, or symbols. No other accessories: no bow, bell, bandana, clothing, or second tag.

Expression default: Bright, attentive, and affectionate. Relaxed amber eyes and a calm closed mouth, head often tilted slightly toward Maya. No angry or worried brow, exposed teeth, tongue out, exaggerated laughter, or human-shaped grin.

Hero pose: Full body sitting upright in three-quarter view beside Maya's step, chest out so the white bib and green collar read clearly, front paws together, striped tail curled around his side with the rings visible. Head turns gently toward the viewer. Keep the pose natural and feline, without raised human-like paws.

Art style: Polished Cartoon — the BookVizion house cast finish (polished storybook cast finish: dimensional cartoon lighting, clear light source, rounded form shading, glossy catchlights). Use softly rounded forms, warm expressive features, gentle painted shading, broad fur shapes, and subtle cream paper texture. Preserve likeness while simplifying detail. Avoid photorealism, realistic individual-hair rendering, glossy 3D surfaces, sharp vector outlines, and exaggerated caricature. Style variants restyle the MEDIUM only via the House Style Kits; his design never changes.

Lighting and palette: Gentle warm diffuse light, delicate cream and ivory highlights, and soft warm-brown shadows. The white chest and front paws remain clearly white; the orange stays warm, not neon. Amber eyes remain vivid and readable without a glow. No dramatic hard light or heavy cinematic contrast.

Portrait framing: Square solo full-body portrait. Include both ears, every visible paw, and the complete striped tail inside the frame with breathing room. Simple LIGHT warm ivory watercolor wash background — pale and airy, very soft faint beige blotches — with a faint grounding shadow (the house portrait standard). No scenery, furniture, props, other characters, text, lettering, logos, or watermark.

Reference priority: The approved Polished Cartoon reference portrait of Biscuit (render order step 1) controls likeness, marking boundaries, and collar treatment in every later render and every style variant. This written recipe overrides any render that drifts from it. Keep the same face, markings, and collar across scenes, poses, expressions, and styles.
