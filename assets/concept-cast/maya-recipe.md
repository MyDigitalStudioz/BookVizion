# Maya — Definitive Hero Character Recipe (BookVizion v6 concept samples, drafted 2026-10-18)

CONCEPT SAMPLE CHARACTER — Maya is an invented character created for BookVizion concept samples only. She is not a real person and is not a client. Every render of Maya is labeled CONCEPT SAMPLE in use.

Species/Identity: Maya is a human girl, about 7 years old, and the hero of the BookVizion concept samples. Preserve this recipe's likeness exactly in every render; only the art style medium changes between renders, never her face, hair, or wardrobe.

Colors: Warm brown skin with a healthy warm undertone. Dark brown eyes with dark pupils and small bright catchlights. Very dark brown, nearly black, curly hair. Mustard-yellow raincoat, cream-and-red striped tee, dark indigo jeans, and red rain boots. Keep skin warm and softly rendered, never ashen, orange, or pale-washed.

Markings: Not applicable as markings; her identity anchors are the two curly hair puffs tied with yellow ties, the gap-tooth smile (a visible gap between the two upper front teeth), and the full wardrobe set listed under Wardrobe. Treat these as marking-equivalents: they appear in every render, in every style.

Signature features: Two round curly puffs, one above each ear, each tied with a small yellow hair tie. Gap-tooth smile — the gap between the two upper front teeth is never closed into a straight smile. Warm expressive dark brown eyes, gently enlarged in cartoon proportion. Softly rounded face with full cheeks, soft straight brows, a small rounded nose, and natural full lips. No freckles, moles, glasses, earrings, or nail polish — none are part of the character.

Proportions/Build: Average build for a 7-year-old, neither slender nor chubby. Head gently enlarged in polished cartoon proportion, hands and feet naturally child-sized. Hair puffs stay full and round, sitting high on the head. Do not age her up or down, slim her into a fashion-doll proportion, or turn her into a toddler or a teenager.

Wardrobe (locked): Mustard-yellow hooded raincoat, worn OPEN so the striped tee always shows, with the HOOD DOWN resting on her back — never up over her hair. Under it, a cream tee with SIX horizontal red stripes across the torso, each stripe about as wide as the cream band between them. Dark indigo jeans, full length. Red rain boots, mid-calf, plain with no logos or patterns. No other clothing, no hat, no scarf, no bag, no jewelry.

Expression default: Warm, open, delighted — a genuine gap-tooth smile with relaxed happy eyes. Her default scene expression is the smile moment: head turned toward Biscuit, smiling at him, book open in her lap. No exaggerated open-mouth laughter, no tongue, no wink, no worried or angry brow, no tears.

Hero pose: Full body seated in three-quarter view on a garden step, picture book open in her lap, head turned gently toward the cat beside her with the gap-tooth smile. In the solo reference portrait she sits in the same seated three-quarter pose on a simple step, book open in her lap, smiling toward the viewer. Keep the pose natural and childlike, without adult posture or fashion posing.

Art style: Polished Cartoon — the BookVizion house cast finish (polished storybook cast finish: dimensional cartoon lighting, clear light source, rounded form shading, glossy catchlights). Use softly rounded forms, warm expressive features, gentle painted shading, and subtle cream paper texture. Preserve likeness while simplifying detail. Avoid photorealism, realistic skin texture or pores, glossy 3D surfaces, sharp vector outlines, and exaggerated caricature. Style variants restyle the MEDIUM only via the House Style Kits; her design never changes.

Lighting and palette: Gentle warm diffuse light, delicate cream and ivory highlights, and soft warm-brown shadows. Skin stays warm brown in shadow as well as light. Eyes remain dark brown and readable without a glow. No dramatic hard light or heavy cinematic contrast.

Portrait framing: Square solo full-body hero portrait. Include the complete hair puffs, hands, and boots inside the frame with breathing room. Simple LIGHT warm ivory watercolor wash background — pale and airy, very soft faint beige blotches — with a faint grounding shadow (the house portrait standard). No scenery, furniture, props beyond the open picture book, other characters, text, lettering, logos, or watermark.

Source photos: none — Maya is invented; the approved reference portrait anchors likeness.

Reference priority: The approved Polished Cartoon reference portrait of Maya (render order step 1) controls likeness, facial proportions, hair, and wardrobe in every later render and every style variant. This written recipe overrides any render that drifts from it. Keep the same face, puffs, smile, and wardrobe across scenes, poses, expressions, and styles.

Reusable render prompt: Definitive solo hero portrait of Maya, an invented 7-year-old girl matching this recipe exactly for likeness. Warm brown skin, dark brown eyes, very dark curly hair in two round puffs tied with small yellow ties, and a gap-tooth smile with a visible gap between the two upper front teeth. Mustard-yellow hooded raincoat worn open with the hood down, cream tee with six red horizontal stripes, dark indigo jeans, red rain boots. Average child build with gently enlarged cartoon head proportion. Full body seated in three-quarter view on a simple garden step, picture book open in her lap, head turned gently toward the viewer with a warm delighted smile. Polished storybook cast finish with dimensional cartoon lighting, softly rounded shapes, gentle painted shading, subtle paper grain, never photorealistic. Square format, complete hair puffs, hands, and boots visible, simple light warm ivory watercolor wash background and faint grounding shadow. No other characters, no accessories, no text, logos, or watermark. Concept sample character.
